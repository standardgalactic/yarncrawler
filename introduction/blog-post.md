## The Hook: The Fog of Information

In our current era of hyper-sensing, we are drowning in data but starving for certainty.

We deploy LiDAR for precision geometry, infrared for thermal signatures, and high-resolution RGB for visual detail, yet these streams rarely agree perfectly. Instead, we are left with a **fog of information**—fragmented, overlapping, and sometimes contradictory snapshots of reality.

Traditionally, this has been treated as a statistical estimation problem. "Digital twins" approximate a best-guess world state.

Yarncrawler changes this.

Using the RSVP field triple:

Φ — scalar density (biomass, infrastructure)  
v — vector flow (transport, movement)  
S — entropy production (complexity, inconsistency)  

the system defines conditions under which the true world-state is not estimated—but *uniquely determined*.

Reconstruction becomes **constraint closure**, not approximation.
